# Complete Engineering Workflow

SureFlow codifies decades of mechanical engineering practices into an intuitive 5-phase loop:

1. **System & Flow Specification**: Defining operating pressures, flow limits, and sizing channel diameters;
2. **Component Placement**: Layout of directional valves, proportional reliefs, and pressure test points with clearance for wrenches and cables;
3. **Internal Channel Routing**: Dual mode: automated AI topology + manual coordinate plane guide;
4. **Process Plugs**: Automatic counter-bores, thread callouts, and O-ring seal seating;
5. **Manufacturing Verification**: Verifying deep-hole aspect ratios ($L/D$) and generating G-code compatible hole schedules.
