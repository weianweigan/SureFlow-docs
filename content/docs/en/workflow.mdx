# Hydraulic Manifold Engineering Workflow

SureFlow codifies fluid power design standards into an intuitive 5-phase engineering closed loop.

---

## Phase 1: Schematic Requirements & Port Sizing

Prior to cavity placement, analyze the hydraulic schematic to identify pressure ratings and flow rates:

- **Port Semantic Standards**:
  - `P` (Pump): High-pressure supply line (typically 16–31.5 MPa; up to 70 MPa in ultra-high pressure applications);
  - `T` (Tank): Low-pressure return line (typically ≤ 1.6 MPa);
  - `A / B`: Actuator service workports;
  - `X / Y`: External pilot control and internal case drain lines;
- **Recommended Fluid Flow Velocity Standards**:

| Line Function | Recommended Velocity | Notes & Design Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Suction Lines** | 0.5 ~ 1.5 m/s | Prevents cavitation and pump starvation |
| **Pressure Supply (P)** | 3.0 ~ 5.5 m/s | Balances line head loss and block envelope volume |
| **Tank Return (T)** | 1.5 ~ 2.5 m/s | Minimizes backpressure and fluid heating |
| **Pilot / Case Drain (X/Y)** | 1.0 ~ 3.0 m/s | Ensures rapid valve spool shifting response |

---

## Phase 2: Stock Sizing & Component Layout

1. **Compact Layout vs. Tooling Ergonomics**:
   - Spacing between adjacent valves must account not only for mounting bolt clearance, but also for hex wrench socket engagement and solenoid coil electrical connector clearance (recommended net clearance ≥ 15 mm);
2. **Mounting Face Partitioning**:
   - **Top Face (+Z)**: Reserved for directional and proportional valves requiring electrical connections and frequent commissioning;
   - **Bottom Face (-Z)**: Mounts to equipment baseplates or hydraulic reservoirs; suited for main P and T flange connections;
   - **Lateral Faces (±X, ±Y)**: Ideal for screw-in cartridge reliefs, counterbalance valves, checks, and test points.

---

## Phase 3: Spatial Channel Routing & Intersections

1. **Orthogonal Intersections**:
   - Ensure connecting bores intersect at 90° whenever possible. Orthogonal intersections create symmetrical elliptical cutting profiles, prevent drill bit wander, and minimize local eddy turbulences;
2. **Effective Cross-Sectional Area**:
   - Maintain intersection areas at least equal to the smaller bore's nominal cross-section to eliminate unwanted throttling.

---

## Phase 4: Construction Plugs & Sealing Types

When internal passages cannot connect directly from one side, cross-drilling construction holes are required:

- **SAE Straight Thread O-Ring Plugs (SAE J1926 / ISO 11926)**:
  - Seals via an elastomeric O-ring compressed into an engineered chamfer; ideal for high-pressure systems with repeatable serviceability;
- **Metric Stud Ends (ISO 6149 / ED Ring)**:
  - Standard throughout European and global machinery platforms;
- **NPT Tapered Pipe Plugs**:
  - Deforms threads for sealing; prone to overtightening stress fractures and galling; avoid in critical high-pressure manifolds.

---

## Phase 5: Manufacturing Process Check & Schedule Output

- **Deep Hole Aspect Ratio ($L/D$) Audit**:
  - Standard twist drills: recommended $L/D \le 10$;
  - Gun drills / deep-hole tooling: up to $L/D \le 20$;
  - Passages exceeding these limits should be split into opposing two-way cross drills;
- **Clearance & Wall Thickness Validation**:
  - Run the live clearance check to ensure all bore-to-bore clearances comply with allowable stress limits;
- **Deliverables Generation**:
  - Export standard ISO 10303 STEP solid models and CSV machining depth schedules for CNC Horizontal Machining Center (HMC) programming.
