# 5-Minute Quick Start

Learn how to create a standard ISO 4401-03 (NG6) directional valve block in under 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Create Project & Define Stock Size
1. Launch SureFlow and click **New Project**;
2. Select base material: `Forged 45# Steel` (or `6061-T6 Aluminum`);
3. Enter stock dimensions ($X = 120\text{ mm}$, $Y = 100\text{ mm}$, $Z = 80\text{ mm}$);
4. An interactive 3D solid is instantly generated in the viewport.

---

## Step 2: Place Standard Valve Cavity
1. Open the **Standard Component Library** on the left panel;
2. Under `ISO 4401`, drag `ISO 4401-03 (CETOP 03 / NG6)` onto the **top face (+Z)**;
3. Center coordinates to $(X=60, Y=50)$;
4. Four mounting bolt holes and four service ports (`P`, `T`, `A`, `B`) will be generated.

---

## Step 3: Add Service Ports
1. Select the bottom or side face;
2. Add two `G 3/8"` threaded ports: $P_0$ (Inlet) and $T_0$ (Tank).

---

## Step 4: AI Channel Auto-Routing
1. Click **AI Auto Route** on the top toolbar;
2. Specify connections: $P_0 \rightarrow P$ and $T_0 \rightarrow T$;
3. Click **Execute**. The internal kernel routes orthogonal shortest paths and adds necessary process plugs in seconds.

---

## Step 5: Verify & Export
1. Check the **Rules Inspector**: 0 clearance violations, safe wall thickness $> 5.2\text{ mm}$;
2. Click **Export** and choose **STEP Solid (.stp)** to save.
