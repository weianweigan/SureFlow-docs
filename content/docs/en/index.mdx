# SureFlow System Overview

**SureFlow** is a professional desktop CAD software engineered for **3D hydraulic manifold block design, cavity layout, and live clearance verification**, serving heavy machinery, machine tools, fluid power systems, and industrial automation.

Traditional hydraulic manifold design heavily relies on tedious 2D CAD projections, manual spatial cross-checking, and error-prone wall thickness estimation. SureFlow provides a unified, parametric 3D workspace from stock sizing and parametric cavity placement to live clearance inspection and high-fidelity STEP solid export.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Parametric Stock & Datum Selection
- **Versatile Blank Geometries**: Rapid parametric definition of rectangular blocks, stepped L-blocks, and T-blocks;
- **Interactive Mounting Faces**: Pick datum faces across Top (+Z), Bottom (-Z), and all lateral sides (±X, ±Y);
- **External STEP Reference**: Import host machine frames or assembly enclosures as translucent references for clash avoidance.

### 2. Standard Cavity Library & Intuitive Placement
- **Standard Screw-in Cartridge Valves**: Built-in cavity templates for industrial standards (Sun, HydraForce, 2-way slip-in cartridge valves);
- **ISO 4401 Subplate Footprints**: Standard mounting patterns for directional and proportional valves (NG6 / CETOP 03, NG10 / CETOP 05);
- **Fluid Ports & Flanges**: SAE straight thread (ISO 11926), Metric ports (ISO 6149), high-pressure SAE J518 flanges, and locating pin bores;
- **Custom Schema Extension**: Expand enterprise-specific valve cavities via clean, parametric JSON definitions.

### 3. Millisecond Clearance & Safety Wall Thickness Inspection
- **Live 3D Collision Detection**: Immediate calculation of spatial clearance between intersecting and non-intersecting channels during drag-and-drop;
- **Pressure-Rated Wall Thickness Check**: Automatic verification based on circuit working pressures (16–31.5 MPa or up to 70 MPa) and material allowable stress;
- **Translucent 3D Section Views**: Inspect channel intersection ellipses and oil flow paths directly in real time.

### 4. Standards-Compliant STEP Solid Export
- **OpenCASCADE (OCCT) Kernel**: Generates standards-compliant ISO 10303 (AP203 / AP214) boundary representation (B-Rep) solids;
- **Seamless Downstream CAD Integration**: Fully compatible with SolidWorks, Siemens NX, PTC Creo, and CATIA for assembly modeling and 2D drafting;
- **Drill Schedules**: Outputs detailed hole coordinates, diameter, machining depth steps, and spotface countersink schedules.
