# 5-Minute Quick Start

This tutorial guides you through designing a standard **ISO 4401-03 (NG6)** directional control valve manifold block, routing fluid passages, and performing live clearance verification in 5 minutes.

---

## Viewport Navigation Gestures

Familiarize yourself with standard 3D mouse controls before beginning layout:

| Gesture | Response | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Left Click** | Pick Face / Select Cavity | Select mounting faces or specific bores to adjust coordinates and depth |
| **Right / Middle Click + Drag** | Free 3D Orbit | Rotate 360° to inspect manifold faces and translucent internal passages |
| **Mouse Wheel Scroll** | Smooth Zoom | Zoom in to check intersection ellipses or zoom out for an overview |
| **Shift + Middle Click + Drag** | Viewport Pan | Translate the viewport center laterally |
| **Double Click / Spacebar** | Reset Camera | Instantly return to standard isometric perspective |

---

## Step 1: Create Project & Define Stock Size

1. Launch SureFlow and select **New Project** on the welcome screen;
2. Choose base material: `Forged 45# Steel` (system defaults to standard allowable tensile stress and density; `6061-T6 Aluminum` or `Ductile Iron QT500` also available);
3. Enter raw stock dimensions (Length × Width × Height):
   - Length $X$ = **120 mm**
   - Width $Y$ = **100 mm**
   - Height $Z$ = **80 mm**
4. Click **Create**; a metallic 3D rectangular solid is instantly generated in the viewport.

---

## Step 2: Place Standard ISO 4401 Valve Footprint

1. Expand the **Standard Cavity Library** in the left toolbox;
2. Navigate to `Subplate Mounting Patterns` and select `ISO 4401-03 (CETOP 03 / NG6)`;
3. Drag the footprint onto the manifold **Top Face (+Z)**;
4. In the Inspector panel, center the placement coordinates to $X = 60\text{ mm}$, $Y = 50\text{ mm}$;
5. SureFlow automatically cuts 4 bolt holes and 4 main port counterbores: `P` (Inlet), `T` (Tank), `A` (Workport A), and `B` (Workport B).

---

## Step 3: Add Threaded Service Ports

1. Orbit the view to a lateral face (such as the -X or -Y face);
2. From the cavity library under `Threaded Ports`, drag a `G 3/8" (BSPP)` straight thread port onto the face;
3. Position service ports $P_0$ and $T_0$:
   - Configure coordinates and drilling depths (e.g., 45 mm depth);
   - The viewport displays translucent cylinders illustrating channel directions in real time.

---

## Step 4: Channel Intersections & Construction Plugs

1. Adjust the depth of top port `P` and side port $P_0$ so they intersect orthogonally inside the manifold;
2. When two ports are on different spatial planes:
   - Introduce an external cross-drilling construction hole;
   - Terminate the opening with a standard `SAE` or `Metric` threaded construction plug (Plugging Head);
3. The viewport calculates the intersection ellipse and highlights fluid flow continuity.

---

## Step 5: Verify Clearances & Export STEP Solid

1. Inspect the **Rules & Geometry Inspector**:
   - **Minimum Wall Thickness**: Displays the absolute closest Euclidean distance between any bore walls (e.g., `5.2 mm`);
   - **Safety Threshold**: Verified against the configured safety limit (`≥ 3.5 mm`); indicator glows green;
   - **Clash Warnings**: Any unintended penetrations or sub-threshold walls are immediately highlighted in amber/red;
2. Click **Export** → **Export STEP Solid (.stp)** to save production-ready B-Rep solids directly for machining or SolidWorks/NX assembly.
