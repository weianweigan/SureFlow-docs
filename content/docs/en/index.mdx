# SureFlow System Overview

**SureFlow** is an industrial-grade collaborative design platform engineered for **intelligent hydraulic manifold blocks**, catering to heavy machinery, aerospace ground support, precision machine tools, and clean energy systems.

It replaces inefficient manual 2D CAD projection drafting, cumbersome clearance validation, and auxiliary cross-drilling with an automated AI topology search and millisecond-level geometric validation.

---

## Core Pillars

### 1. AI Channel Topology Optimization
SureFlow leverages 3D spatial graph search and fluid resistance minimization algorithms:
- Automatically sizes passage cross-sections based on rated flows and pressures across ports (P, T, A, B, X, Y);
- Explores shortest 3D routes inside raw stock, favoring orthogonal passages to minimize right-angle bend head losses;
- Minimizes external process plugging holes, eliminating leak points at the source.

### 2. Real-time Wall Thickness & Clearance Verification
- **Dynamic 3D clearance monitoring**: Millisecond recalculation of shortest Euclidean distances between arbitrary spatial bores and surfaces;
- **Pressure-rated thickness safety**: Color-coded warnings based on working pressure (16–31.5 MPa or up to 70 MPa ultra-high pressure) and material yield strength.

### 3. Standards-Compliant Industrial Export
- **Lossless STEP export**: Compliant with ISO 10303 (AP203, AP214, AP242) for seamless import into SolidWorks, Siemens NX, Creo, and CATIA;
- **Machining depth schedules**: Automated drill schedules ready for direct execution on Horizontal Machining Centers (HMC).
